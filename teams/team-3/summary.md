# team number 3 (Team 3)

One-liner: Art-helper helps museum visitors to gain updated information by scanning an NFC tag.

Tool used: Microsoft Copilot powered by GPT-5 chat model

## Problem

- Blind and visually impaired people.
- Dyslexic people.
- Tourists and foreigners.
- School classes and museum visitors in general.
- They do not get the same experience as other visitors.
- Existing solutions often use borrowed audio headsets with poor sound quality.

## Money

- Museums pay for NFC tags and access to the service.
- Danske Museer funds development and maintenance through a subscription model.
- Buy-in cost: 1,000,000 DKK.
- NFC tags: 5 DKK per tag.
- Subscription: 150,000 DKK per month for all museums in Denmark.
- Benefits: more eco-friendly, easier updates, more inclusive, potential to attract more visitors.

## Security

- Threats mentioned: GDPR issues, DoS attacks, data leaks.
- Goal: compromise the system and gain access to user data.
- Mitigations: EU data storage, hardened security, DoS protection.
- Abuse case: access to user information about visual impairment or dyslexia.
- MISSING: concrete attackers and attack methods.

## Critical infrastructure

- Scenario: attackers gain access to the app and use audio channels to distribute propaganda.
- Possible consequence: terror threats or harmful messages sent directly to users.
- Mitigation: secure and separate databases, data and functions.
