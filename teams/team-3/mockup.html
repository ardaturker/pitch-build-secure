Art-helper

Art-helper helps museum visitors to gain updated information by scanning an NFC tag.

Problem

  • Blind and visually impaired visitors struggle to access artwork information.
  • Dyslexic users have difficulties reading descriptions.
  • Tourists and school classes need easier access to information.
  • Current audio solutions use borrowed headsets with poor sound quality.

Solution

  • NFC tag on every artwork.
  • Visitors scan with the Art-helper app.
  • Audio descriptions and updated information on demand.
  • Users listen through their own headphones.

Pricing

  • Buy-in: 1,000,000 DKK.
  • NFC tags: 5 DKK per tag.
  • Subscription: 150,000 DKK per month for all museums in Denmark.
  • Museums and cultural organisations fund the service.

Security & Critical Infrastructure

  • Risks: GDPR issues, DoS attacks and data leaks.
  • Mitigations: EU data storage, hardened security and DoS protection.
  • Risk scenario: attackers misuse audio channels for propaganda or threats.
  • Additional protection through database and function separation.
