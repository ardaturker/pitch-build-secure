Blind Glasses

Problem

Visually impaired people cannot see their surroundings and often rely on support animals, assistance from other people, assistive tools, and phone text-to-speech applications.

Solution

AI-powered glasses that help visually impaired people understand what is in front of them and navigate more independently using audio feedback.

Pricing

  • Upfront cost: 8,000 DKK
  • AI subscription: 700 DKK/month
  • Government covers most costs (around 80%)

Security & Critical Infrastructure

  • Threat actors: other countries, people with an agenda against the company, data thieves, and blackmailers
  • Risks: false navigation, misleading information, data misuse, and blackmail
  • Critical infrastructure: the system could become a security risk if compromised
  • Team decision: not intended for critical infrastructure environments
