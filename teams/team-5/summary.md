# Blind Glasses (Team 5)

One-liner: Blind Glasses helps visually impaired people solve navigation and awareness challenges by using AI-powered glasses that describe surroundings and assist with movement.

Tool used: Microsoft Copilot (GPT-5 chat model)

## Problem

- Users: visually impaired people.
- They cannot see their surroundings, making everyday activities more difficult.
- Current solutions include support animals, assistance from other people, assistive tools, and text-to-speech phone applications.

## Money

- Main customer: government.
- Product aims to increase independence for visually impaired people.
- Upfront cost: 8,000 DKK.
- AI subscription: 700 DKK per month.
- Government covers approximately 80% of costs.
- Team believes it is valuable compared with alternatives such as service dogs and ongoing assistance.

## Security

- Threat actors:
  - Other countries.
  - People with an agenda against the company.
  - People who want data for training other AI systems.
  - Blackmailers.
- Risks:
  - Incorrect navigation instructions.
  - Misleading descriptions of situations.
  - Data misuse or public disclosure.
  - Blackmail.
  - False accusations.

## Critical infrastructure

- If compromised, the system could become a security risk.
- Incorrect guidance could lead to harm or abuse.
- Team position: the solution should not be used in critical infrastructure environments.
